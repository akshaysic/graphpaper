# SectionIntro

A section header: one `display-xl` headline and an `intro` paragraph, left-aligned or centred.

**You provide** `title`, the intro as children, `align`, and `introPosition` (`above` when the section opens with a claim).

Headline in Title Case, intro in sentence case, three lines and 46 characters wide at most, `space-m` apart. Works on light and dark bands unchanged.
