# FooterSlab

The closing section: a `sage` slab with one rounded corner, a two-part statement, the name, link columns, the copyright line, and an extra-large CtaTab in the bottom-right corner joined to the slab by a connector.

**You provide** `statement` (two halves joined by a plus), `name` (your mark or wordmark, set in `display-l`), `about` (one sentence, `small`), `columns` of NavLinks, `legal` (`label-wide`) and `cta`.

The slab sits on a dark ground, stops short of the right edge and rounds its top-right corner with `radius-xl`. Everything on it is `on-light`. The connector is measured and redrawn on resize.
